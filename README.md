### 📊 Featured Projects

#### [Local Beauty Industry Market Analysis & Lead Pipeline](./beauty-salon-market-analysis)
* **Objective:** Quantify the market impact of digital infrastructure and identify addressable business opportunities within local service markets.
* **Pipeline:** Built an automated ETL pipeline using Python (Playwright) and Outscraper to extract, clean, and structure directory data across 119 local businesses into a normalized dataset.
* **Modeling & Visualization:** Developed an interactive Power BI dashboard featuring dynamic DAX conditional formatting (`SWITCH` logic) and automated cross-filtering.
* **Key Findings:** Revealed that businesses with dedicated websites average 173 reviews versus 49 for social-only footprints, while identifying an 85% market gap for targeted outreach.
